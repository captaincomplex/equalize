Sound

Sound

On your iPhone, open the AirPlay picker (Control Centre, or the speaker icon in Spotify) and tick your Sonos and Equalize. The Pi makes no sound; it only watches. Demo plays a built-in pattern for testing the panel.

Spotify

Spotify speakers

In the Spotify app, choose Equalize as the device. The music plays on the speakers ticked here, and the panel draws it in step. AirPlay still works as before.
